About Johnstown Water Damage Pros

A 24/7 water damage restoration line for Johnstown and nearby Cambria County towns, built around clear scope, measured drying and straight answers.

☎ (814) 358-8169

What we do

Johnstown Water Damage Pros answers calls about water damage around the clock and arranges restoration professionals to start extraction, drying and repair work. Our service pages cover nineteen specific sources of water damage, from a burst pipe to a sewage backup, so you can read what applies to your situation before you call.

The business address is listed in the footer of every page, and the phone number is the same everywhere on the site. We would rather tell you plainly what we do than dress it up.

What we will not promise

We do not promise an arrival time, because Johnstown weather, hills and call volume change what is possible on any given night. We do not promise that an insurance claim will be approved, and we do not quote a price before someone has seen the damage. We offer same-day response when it is available and explain scope and price before work starts.

We also do not publish reviews, ratings or customer counts on this site. If those ever appear, they will be real and verifiable.

The standards we point to

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published industry standard for water damage restoration, and we point to it as a reference only. The standard covers how water is categorized, how drying goals are set and how progress is documented. You are welcome to ask any restoration company, including this one, whether its crews work to it and how they record their readings.

How a call is handled

A call to the 24/7 line is answered by a dispatcher who asks a few direct questions. Where is the water coming from, has it been stopped and what has it reached. From those answers they decide what equipment and crew size make sense and give you immediate steps while help is arranged.

We say plainly when we are unsure. If a problem needs a plumber, an electrician or the utility before drying can begin, we tell you that first.

What the work looks like

The sequence is the same for most jobs. Stop the source, remove standing water, open and dry what is wet and document readings until the structure reaches its goal. Then comes cleaning and repair. You can see each step described on pages such as water extraction and structural drying.

Scope and price are explained before work starts. If the scope changes, you hear about it before extra work is done.

What we do not do

We do not offer mold remediation, and we say so rather than imply it. We do not make plumbing repairs on the supply side of a leak unless a plumber is arranged, and we do not give legal or coverage advice. For those, a plumber, an attorney or your insurer is the right call.

Our blog explains many of these topics in plain language, from wall and drywall repair to insurance questions. It is a good place to start if you are not in an emergency.

How to reach us

The phone is the fastest way. The number is the same on every page, and the address and email appear in the footer and on the area pages and contact page. For anything urgent, call rather than write.

We look forward to being useful, whether that is a quick question about a leak or a full drying job.

Why the local detail matters

Johnstown is a river city. The Stonycreek, Little Conemaugh and Conemaugh rivers meet here, and the city lived through floods in 1889, 1936 and 1977. That history shapes how we talk about basements, sump pumps and flood insurance, and it is why our flood damage cleanup page tells you to check the FEMA flood map for your own address.

Our approach to documentation

Good records protect you. We take photos, record moisture readings and keep a written scope so you and your insurer can see what was found and done. If a reading does not reach its goal, we say so and keep drying rather than close things up.

You are welcome to ask for copies of readings at any point during a job.

How to judge any restoration company

Ask about response, standards, documentation, insurance and what is not included. Our blog post on choosing a company lists the questions and the answers to listen for. We would rather you ask all of them than sign something you do not understand.

If you are weighing quotes, compare scopes line by line, not just totals.

Questions we are glad to answer

Ask us how a job would run, what a typical drying goal looks like, who handles the rebuild and what happens if the scope changes. Ask what the crew will not do. Plain answers up front prevent most disputes later.

If we are not the right fit for your situation, we will tell you, and we will point you to the right kind of professional.
