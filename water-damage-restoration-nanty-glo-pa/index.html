A coal town in a creek valley

Nanty Glo is a borough of 2,511 people in Cambria County, with ZIP code 15943. It lies in the valley of the South Branch of Blacklick Creek, at about 1,709 feet, and it is one mile north of US 22. Johnstown is about 12 miles to the southwest and Ebensburg is about 7 miles east. The 24/7 emergency line serves the borough every day of the year.

Valley towns collect water. When water is spreading in your home, emergency dispatch is available around the clock with crews dispatched as availability allows, and the steps below help while you wait.

The borough and its creek

The borough began in the 1890s as a lumber camp called Glenglade, was renamed Nant-y-glo in 1901 and was incorporated in 1918. The name is Welsh and means stream of coal. Commercial mining began in 1896, and the Heisley Mine, opened in 1915, was the largest. A town that grew with coal mining in those decades likely has older housing, so ask the crew to check aging supply lines and old plumbing.

Black Lick Creek winds through town, and a 2024 state inspection raised concerns about the borough's levee system. A $100,000 Greenways, Trails and Recreation Program grant was announced in May 2024 for the first phase of levee work. Levee projects take time, so check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask your insurer about flood coverage now.

ZIP codes around Nanty Glo

Nanty Glo uses ZIP code 15943. Nearby ZIP codes we serve are 15960 for Twin Rocks, 15948 for Revloc and 15922 for Belsano, along the roads that run out of the valley. The Ghost Town Trail starts in Nanty Glo and follows old rail corridors, so homes near the trail may sit close to old mine and stream channels.

Permit questions for rebuilds go to the borough. The City of Johnstown has its own rules, so do not assume they apply here. If you are unsure, ask the borough office.

What the crew does

The crew extracts water, maps moisture, removes wet materials and dries the structure with air movers and dehumidifiers. Wet drywall and insulation come out first if the water was contaminated. Scope and price are explained before work starts, and readings are logged for your insurer.

If you live closer to Johnstown, see Seward, which is covered by the same line. We never promise an arrival time, since conditions on PA 271 and US 22 vary, but same-day response is offered based on crew availability.

Safety checklist and standards

The drying and water category terms used here come from ANSI/IICRC S500, the industry standard for professional water damage restoration. For claims, the Pennsylvania Insurance Department advises photographing damage before cleanup and keeping receipts.

  • Stay out of moving or deep water, and keep away from downed lines.
  • Do not enter a basement if water is near the panel, outlets or a furnace.
  • Call the utility if you smell gas or see sparks.
  • Wear boots and gloves for any contact with floodwater.
