Water damage in Elton: a crossroads community

Elton is an unincorporated community in Cambria County with ZIP code 15934. It sits at the junction of Pennsylvania Routes 160 and 756, about 7.1 miles east-southeast of Johnstown, at an elevation near 2,054 feet. The 24/7 emergency line covers Elton every day of the year, and when water is spreading, emergency dispatch is available around the clock.

Jurisdiction and permits in Elton

Elton is not a borough, so it has no borough office. Permit and zoning questions go to the township and Cambria County, and the Laurel Municipal Inspection Agency, which serves the City of Johnstown at (814) 471-0424, may not be the office for your address. Ask the county where to apply before a major rebuild.

The same is true of water and sewer. Some rural homes have wells and septic systems, so a water problem may not involve a public utility at all. If you do not know, check your water bill.

What the crew does

The crew extracts water, maps moisture with meters and a thermal camera and removes wet materials that cannot be dried. Air movers and dehumidifiers run until readings meet the goal, and the scope and price are explained before work starts. The drying is explained on the structural drying page.

Neighbors in Salix are about 2 miles away by straight line and covered by the same line.

Planning ahead in a small community

Elton is a small community, and nearby services can take longer to reach than in a larger town. That makes preparation worth the effort. Keep the number of the water provider or well service, your plumber and the restoration line together in your phone.

If your home uses a private well and pressure tank, learn where the pump breaker and shutoff are. A failed pressure tank or pump line can flood a utility room just like a burst pipe.

After the emergency crew leaves

Ask what readings the crew is aiming for and when they will return. Keep doors and the thermostat as they recommend, and avoid moving equipment. Take notes on dates and times so your insurer has a clear record.

Questions about county permits for the rebuild go to the township and county, so ask before work begins.

Cost and insurance

Cost depends on the water category, the area affected and the drying days. Want your real number? Call (814) 358-8169.

Sudden damage is often covered, but gradual leaks usually are not. Read your policy or ask your insurer.

Safety checklist and standards

The drying and water category terms used here come from ANSI/IICRC S500, the industry standard for professional water damage restoration. For claims, the Pennsylvania Insurance Department advises photographing damage before cleanup and keeping receipts.

  • Stay out of moving or deep water, and keep away from downed lines.
  • Do not enter a basement if water is near the panel, outlets or a furnace.
  • Call the utility if you smell gas or see sparks.
  • Wear boots and gloves for any contact with floodwater.
