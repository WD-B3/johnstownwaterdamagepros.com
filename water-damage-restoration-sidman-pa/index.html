What Sidman is, and why it matters for water damage

Sidman is an unincorporated community and census-designated place, not a borough, so there is no borough office to call about repairs. The CDP covers about one square mile of land. Until the 2010 census it was part of the combined St. Michael-Sidman area, and it was split into two places after that, which is why older records may show both names.

In practice this means permit and zoning questions go to Adams Township and Cambria County rather than to a borough hall. When you hire for a rebuild, ask the township office what applies to your address before work starts.

ZIP codes around Sidman

Sidman uses ZIP code 15955. Nearby ZIP codes we serve include 15956 for South Fork, 15942 for Mineral Point, 15945 for Parkhill and 15930 for Dunlo. The 1889 flood began when the South Fork Dam failed, holding back Conemaugh Lake about 14 miles up the Conemaugh River from Johnstown, so the history of this stretch of Cambria County is tied to water.

The lesson for homeowners is practical. The valleys here funnel runoff quickly, so after a heavy rain check sump pumps, window wells and downspouts, and check the FEMA flood map for your address.

Safety checklist and standards

The drying and water category terms used here come from ANSI/IICRC S500, the industry standard for professional water damage restoration. For claims, the Pennsylvania Insurance Department advises photographing damage before cleanup and keeping receipts.

  • Stay out of moving or deep water, and keep away from downed lines.
